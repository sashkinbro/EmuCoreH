Local additions: frontend/jgenesis-libretro, core-jgenesis-android Gradle wrapper.
Local backend patches: expose Genesis battery-save flush and add SMS/GG shutdown
save flush. All modifications remain in the source tree under the upstream GPLv3.
Cargo.lock records exact third-party dependency versions; their original license
notices remain in the corresponding dependency sources distributed by Cargo.

No Sega CD or 32X BIOS/firmware is included. The local s32x-core patch removes
the embedded 32X boot/security ROM data and requires validated user-supplied
firmware at runtime; the security-program detector still verifies cartridge
data by CRC32. This integration does not claim release clearance.
